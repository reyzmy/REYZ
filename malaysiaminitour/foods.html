Malaysian Foods

Malaysian cuisine consists of cooking traditions and practices found in Malaysia, and reflects the multi-ethnic makeup of its population.

NASI LEMAK

Nasi lemak is Malaysia’s national dish. It consists of fragrant rice cooked in coconut milk, served with sambal (spicy chili paste), boiled egg, peanuts, anchovies, and cucumber.

ROTI CANAI

Roti canai is a soft, flaky flatbread that is usually served with curry or dhal (lentil gravy). It is a popular breakfast and snack in Malaysia.

CHAR KOEY TEOW

Char kuey teow is a stir-fried flat rice noodle dish cooked with prawns, eggs, bean sprouts, and soy sauce. It has a smoky flavor and is one of Malaysia’s most loved street foods.

LAKSA

Laksa is a spicy noodle soup made with coconut milk or tamarind, depending on the region. It’s flavorful, aromatic, and often topped with prawns or chicken.

NASI KANDAR

Nasi kandar is a rice dish served with a variety of curries and side dishes such as fried chicken, squid, or vegetables. It is known for its rich, spicy flavors.

MANOK PANSUH

Manok pansuh is a traditional Sarawak dish made by cooking chicken with herbs inside bamboo. It has a unique aroma and taste from the bamboo and spices.

APAM BALIK

Apam balik is a sweet Malaysian pancake filled with crushed peanuts, sugar, and sometimes corn. It’s crispy on the outside and soft inside.

OTAK-OTAK

Otak-otak is a grilled fish paste mixed with spices and coconut milk, wrapped in banana leaves. It has a soft texture and smoky flavor.

CENDOL

Cendol is a sweet iced dessert made with coconut milk, palm sugar syrup, and green rice flour jelly. It’s a popular treat on hot days in Malaysia.
